# DEBT RUNNER

Unity 6 / URP 2D top-down roguelite prototype led by a 4-person student team and selected for the Academy of Art University Spring Show 2026.

> A playable prototype built from concept to production-ready presentation in four weeks.

## My role

**Game Project Lead / Producer + Gameplay Engineer**

- Planned scope, milestones, task ownership, and production priorities.
- Wrote and maintained the GDD and coordinated art, UI, and gameplay work.
- Implemented and connected 7 core gameplay systems in Unity.
- Ran QA iterations for tutorial flow, combat readability, UI clarity, round pacing, and reward feedback.
- Used playtest and instructor feedback to prioritize fixes and move the project toward a playable showcase build.

## Project snapshot

- **Team:** 4-person student team
- **Engine:** Unity 6 with URP
- **Language:** C#
- **Format:** 2D top-down bullet-heaven roguelite
- **Delivery:** Playable prototype completed in 4 weeks
- **Recognition:** Selected for the Academy of Art University Spring Show 2026

## Core systems

The prototype connects game-state flow, player movement, auto-target combat, enemy spawning, projectile logic, XP and level-up rewards, settlement flow, shop loop, and game-over handling.

## Why it matters

DEBT RUNNER demonstrates production coordination, gameplay systems integration, iterative QA, and the ability to turn ambiguous creative requirements into a scoped, testable prototype.
